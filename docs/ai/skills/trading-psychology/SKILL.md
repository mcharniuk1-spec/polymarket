# Trading Psychology

## Purpose

Reduce avoidable paper-trading mistakes caused by confirmation bias, overconfidence, loss chasing, anchoring, recency bias, and narrative attachment.

## Workflow

1. Write the base-rate estimate before reading supportive news.
2. Search for disconfirming evidence and contradiction flags.
3. Run a short pre-mortem: how could this bet lose despite looking attractive?
4. Cap stake size before looking at recent wins/losses.
5. After settlement, label mistakes as model, context, liquidity, stake size, wording, or variance.

## Required Checks

- What evidence would change the view?
- Is the confidence justified by past calibration?
- Is the market wording objective enough?
- Is this correlated with existing paper exposure?
- Is the stake a system rule or an emotional reaction?

## Rule

Psychology controls should lower or block paper exposure; they should never justify higher risk without new evidence.
