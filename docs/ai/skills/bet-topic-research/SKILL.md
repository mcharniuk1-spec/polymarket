# Bet Topic Research

## Purpose

Research one candidate bet or one topic separately from the global/category news analyzer. The result should be an auditable brief that can be attached to a paper decision.

## Workflow

1. Identify category, market title/topic, actors, outcome, market URL, and settlement notes.
2. Select source registry entries for global, Polymarket, and category-specific evidence.
3. Generate source-specific queries without live fetching by default.
4. Summarize fixture-backed evidence and mark live evidence as pending.
5. Assign source coverage, contradiction, staleness, and resolution-risk flags.
6. Produce a paper-only research conclusion: research-ready, watchlist, reject, or needs primary source.

## Required Output

- candidate or topic identity
- planned source queries
- fixture evidence items
- global context score
- category context score
- bet research score
- contradiction, staleness, and resolution flags
- next evidence needed

## Safety

Do not scrape private pages, bypass protections, store credentials, or place trades.
