# Decision: Research-Only Boundary

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

Keep the Polymarket MVP limited to local research, historical backtesting, paper-trading logs, and performance reporting.

## Out Of Scope

- Real-money betting.
- Wallet actions.
- Automated order execution.
- Credential storage.
- Exchange trading execution.
